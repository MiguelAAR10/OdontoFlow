## Merge danger / risks
- `_execute` catches only `AppError`. Another exception from an executor (e.g. `IntegrityError`) gives a 500 and
  leaves the row in `approved`, holding the dedupe slot. No re-drive job exists yet.
- Dedupe-race retry: if no row is found after the unique violation, `_settle_create` raises `AttributeError` (500).
- ORM `__table_args__` omit several CHECKs and `ix_agent_proposals_inbox` that 0021 creates (model drift only).
- `evidence` JSONB has no size/depth limit. `next_follow_up_on` accepts past dates (fails at execution, not create).
- Inbox re-validates stored payloads with `load_args`; tightening an args model later can break old-row reads.
- Unmapped agent names resolve to `agent_key` `reception` until B4 (a collections agent shows as reception).
- 0021 migration test does not assert the `system` role grants or their downgrade removal.
- Not done: expiry sweep, re-drive of stuck `approved` rows, domain events, inventory kinds (C2), waitlist_offer (C3).
